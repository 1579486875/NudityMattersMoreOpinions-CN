I hope this message isn't unwelcome. I've been using your combined edition for a good
while now, and over the past months I've put together a Simplified Chinese translation
for it — largely because a fair number of people in the Chinese community play with it

Repo: https://github.com/1579486875/NudityMattersMoreOpinions-CN

The idea was to keep it as one pack covering the three setups people actually run,
rather than three separate translations that drift apart:

- the 1.7 combined edition
- 1.6 Nudity Matters More
- 1.6 Nudity Matters More opinions

Most of the text turned out to be ordinary DefInjected, which the game loads without
any help from me — currently 17,896 leaves across 49 files. Three other channels
needed a different approach, which I mention because they're the parts that could
plausibly interact with your own code:

The combined edition builds its opinion lists in C# rather than reading them out of
XML, so nothing in the normal translation path reaches them. A small DLL injects those
at runtime instead — 5,214 containers, 125,750 entries across 47 files. Each container
is checked against the original def's list length when it loads, so if a later update
of yours adds or removes an entry, it fails loudly rather than quietly mismatching.

Around 103 strings in the enhanced tab are hard-coded in C#, and the UI labels your
code builds at runtime are the same, so those are swapped with a Harmony transpiler.
The transpilers only rewrite string literals; they don't touch control flow, and each
one bails out quietly if its target method isn't there.

I also diffed the finished result against your originals rather than trusting it by
eye: 5,082 OpinionDef_SexPart defs, 123,021 body lines, nothing missing and nothing
left empty. If a later version of yours adds new text, the coverage check on startup
will say so in the log instead of silently showing English.

The pack doesn't declare your mods as dependencies. If neither is loaded it just sits
there doing nothing rather than throwing errors, so it's safe to leave enabled.

No action needed from you at all. I mainly wanted it on your radar in case it's useful
to point people at. If you'd like to link it from the thread that would of course be
welcome, but there's genuinely no obligation. And if anything about how I've packaged
or presented it causes you concern — the naming, the DLL, the way it attaches — please
just say so and I'll change it, or take the whole thing down if that's what you'd
prefer. I'd much rather ask than assume.

Thanks for the mod. It's an enormous amount of writing and it's consistently good,
