One other thing, in case it's useful. While testing I hit a case where the opinions
tab doesn't show up for some race mods, even though everything else about the mod
works fine — nudity still gets covered, streaking still drops mood, opinions still
generate. It traces back to `ITab_Pawn_NMMOpinions.IsVisible` depending on
`rjw.xxx.can_do_loving`, and RJW deciding "mechanoid" by checking whether the race's
`FleshType` defName contains `Android` as a substring. Any humanlike race whose
FleshType name happens to contain that is treated as a mechanoid, `can_do_loving`
bails on its first line, and the tab never appears.

Age isn't involved — I edited a pawn from 16 to 20, saved and reloaded, tab still
gone.

I wrote it up properly with the decompiled details and a one-line change that would
avoid it, if you want me to send it over. Not urgent, and not asking you to drop
everything for it.
